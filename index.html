To My Two Favourite People,

If I could give you both one thing in this world, it would be the ability to see yourselves through my eyes. Maybe then you would understand how much you mean to me and how lucky I feel to be your child. Your love has been my safest place, your sacrifices have shaped my life, and your happiness will always be one of my biggest wishes.

Even though miles separate us right now, please remember that distance can never make me feel any less close to you. I miss the little moments, the conversations, the laughter and simply having you both around. I wish I could be there to celebrate this beautiful day beside you, but until that moment comes, I'm sending all my love across the miles.

Thank you for showing me what unconditional love looks like. I hope you continue holding each other's hands, creating memories and choosing each other every single day. No matter how old I grow or how far life takes us, I'll always be your little one. I love you both more than words could ever express.

Forever your child, ♡
